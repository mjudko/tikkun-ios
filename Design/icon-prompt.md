# Tikkun icon concept

Generated with the built-in image generation tool. This is a design concept, not yet wired into the Xcode app icon catalog.

## Prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: finished iOS app icon for Tikkun Koreh, a Torah reading practice app. Create one square 1024x1024 full-bleed opaque icon, not a mockup. A beautifully restrained open Torah scroll, front-facing, with two substantial dark ink-colored wooden rollers framing warm ivory parchment; two small columns of abstract dark horizontal writing strokes evoke Torah columns without any readable scripture or lettering. Elegant sculptural paper, extremely subtle relief and soft shadows, strong simple silhouette, warm parchment background with a very subtle tonal gradient. Palette ivory, parchment, nearly black warm brown; quiet scholarly feel. Centered bold composition occupying about 72% of canvas, generous balanced margin, legible at small home-screen size. Premium native iOS visual design, crisp precise edges, minimal details. No words, no letters, no badges, no surrounding device, no border, no pre-rounded corners, no transparency. Full square artwork to its edges; iOS will apply its own corner mask.
